Yoto labels on TownStix US-10

Click an empty slot or drop images on it. "Add images…" fills empty slots in order. Inside a picture: scroll to zoom, drag to move, double-click to replace. Click the title area to type. When printing, set scale to 100% (Actual size), margins None, paper Letter. Test on plain paper first and hold it against a label sheet; use the offsets to correct a small shift. Each label is 4 x 2 in; the card zone is 3.375 x 2 in (a Yoto card), so trim at the dashed line.
